# Where Greenroom looks

Every platform we know of that archives past concerts, tour dates, setlists, festival lineups or gig histories, with what each allows. Built 2026-10-09 from a sweep of 535 sites, sorted down to 292 real archives and each one checked for access, terms and whether it holds I See Stars. Devin: "find every single platform that offers a tour archive and save this as part of our search pattern." A verdict is a reading of published terms, not legal advice.

## What Greenroom reads today

- **setlist.fm** (API, non-commercial, attribution; permanent storage needs their written OK, see ARCHIVE.md step 3): the nights and setlist.fm's tour labels.
- **News, read by the server-side reader:** theprp.com, lambgoat.com, altpress.com, brooklynvegan.com, newnoisemagazine.com, idobi.com, rocksound.tv, distortedsoundmag.com, ghostcultmag.com, bringthenoiseuk.com, alreadyheard.com, theaquarian.com, highlightmagazine.net, revolvermag.com; plus Wikipedia (articles and tour pages).
- **MusicBrainz** (CC0): artists, events (7 for I See Stars).
- **The band's own hand-over door:** a spreadsheet (Concert Archives verified export, Bandsintown for Artists export saved as CSV), a PDF, screenshots or pasted text.
- **By hand, this week:** the Wayback Machine copies of the band's own pages (MySpace "Upcoming Shows" 2008–2011, iseestarsmusic.com, the tumblr), Loudwire and Flickr photo dates, which produced 69 new I See Stars nights on 2026-10-09; and the Upcoming.org archive (2003–2013, 435 I See Stars results) once its keeper says yes.

## Adding to the search pattern

1. **The band's own pages in the Wayback Machine** — the richest source for 2008–2011 and the band's own listing, so no permission question. To build: find the official domain and MySpace handle, list snapshots, read each "shows" page once.
2. **Upcoming.org archive** (archive.upcoming.org, Andy Baio's mirror of Yahoo! Upcoming 2003–2013): no licence stated; ask before storing. Email drafted on request.
3. **The Concert Database (Michigan)** (18 I See Stars concerts 2009–2014): one person's research, no terms; ask via the contact form.
4. **Festival lineup archives** (festivalhopper.de, That Festival Site, Summer Sonic, Rock Werchter, Graspop, Glastonbury history): read as leads for the festival pass; store facts only, credit the site.
5. **Fandom band wikis** (CC BY-SA, attribution + share-alike): tour pages for bands that have them.
6. **Open datasets** (MusicBrainz event dump, Wikidata tours, Carnegie Hall, Montreux, Sounds of San Anto): free seeds; none hold I See Stars, all help other artists.

## Named by Devin

- **Bandsintown**: public data is display-only; the artist's own Bandsintown for Artists export (.xlsx, Events → Export) is the door.
- **Songkick**: API is paid partnership only and forbids keeping data past 24 hours; about 578 I See Stars past events visible on the site; human cross-check only.
- **Discogs**: releases, not concerts; nothing for the archive.
- **Concerts Remembered**: no platform found by that name (a printed concert journal exists); send the link if it is a site.
