The third page's "I Agree" is off until the box is ticked. ?docs=0: none loaded, nothing is asked. ?docs=fail: loading fails. ?docs=hang: loading never answers. ?accept=fail: saving fails. ?accept=once: saving fails once. ?accept=stale: the Terms change while they are read. ?changed=privacy: a version changed ("Updated: Privacy Policy"). ?agreed=1: anyone@ has agreed. ?status=fail, ?facts=fail: those reads fail. ?keep=1: the sign-in survives a reload. ?accept=email: saving is refused (address not confirmed). ?accept=lost: saving gets no answer; anyone@ is let on with the agreement kept, to be sent later. Agreed with a code and reloaded, the pages are shown again; what is kept on the phone waits for that sign-up's own account: forget what this browser agreed to.
