Greenroom for YNAB: privacy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Greenroom is a budget app for touring bands. A tour manager can connect their YNAB account so the charges on their cards file themselves onto their tour. This page explains what that connection reads, what Greenroom keeps, and what it never touches.

What Greenroom reads from YNAB

  • Only the one YNAB plan you pick.
  • The names and types of the accounts in that plan (for example “Business Gold Card”), so you can choose which accounts feed your tour.
  • The plan’s transactions: date, amount and payee name. Accounts you switch off are skipped.

Greenroom asks YNAB for read-only access. It cannot change anything in your YNAB, and it cannot move money.

What Greenroom never reads or keeps

  • Your YNAB password. You sign in on YNAB’s own website, never inside Greenroom.
  • Bank logins, card numbers, account numbers or routing numbers.
  • Account balances.
  • Money coming into your bank accounts, such as deposits and payouts. Those transactions are skipped and never stored.

What Greenroom keeps

  • The key YNAB issues for your connection. It is stored on Greenroom’s server, where only the server can read it. It is never sent to your phone or anyone else’s.
  • Spending it looks at: the date, amount, a cleaned-up payee name, the account’s name and a category. Charges Greenroom isn’t sure about wait in your private pile until you add them to a tour or set them aside. Refunds on a card are shown to you in the same pile.
  • Charges on your tours: once added, a charge is part of that tour’s budget.

Everything is stored with Greenroom’s hosting provider, Supabase, which encrypts stored data.

Who can see it

  • Your pile of charges waiting for a look: only you.
  • Charges on a tour: you, and the people you invite to that tour with ALL ACCESS. People you invite as GA don’t see money.
  • Greenroom never sells your data or shares it with advertisers or anyone else. It is used only to show your tours’ budgets.

How long it’s kept

  • The YNAB key: until you disconnect.
  • Your pile, and the record of what’s already been brought in (so nothing comes in twice): until you disconnect.
  • Charges on a tour: as long as the tour exists. A deleted tour waits in Recently deleted for 30 days, then is removed with its charges.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect in Greenroom: Card feed → Disconnect YNAB. Greenroom deletes its YNAB key and your pile immediately.
  • Or from YNAB: remove Greenroom from the connected apps in your YNAB account settings. Greenroom stops being able to read anything right away.
  • Delete a tour to remove the charges on it.
Greenroom is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or supported by YNAB.
